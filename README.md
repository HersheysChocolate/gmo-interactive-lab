# GMO Interactive Lab

Open the [GMO Interactive Lab website](https://hersheyschocolate.github.io/gmo-interactive-lab/) in a browser.

## How to use the website

1. On **Overview**, choose **Explore the lab**.
2. In **DNA & Crop Lab**, choose **Insect defense**, **Water saving**, or **Extra nutrition**.
3. Change the crop and adjust insect risk, water available, or trait effect. The example scores and possible benefits and downsides update as you explore.
4. Open **Examples** to see real crop examples, or **Sources** to read the research behind them.
5. Turn on **Exhibition mode** for a presentation-friendly view. Choose **Reset demo** to restore the starting settings.

The DNA marker is a teaching visual. Crop scores are made-up examples, not real harvest results, nutrient measurements, or yield predictions.
